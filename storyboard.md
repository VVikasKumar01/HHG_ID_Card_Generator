# UI Storyboard & Experience Flow — Hacker House Goa 2026

## Act I: Arrival & Orientation
1. **Scene 01 — Hero Reveal**:
   - Camera lands on `2:47PM STUDIO` pixel header.
   - Aerial Goa beach photography seamlessly blended into palm green backdrop.
   - Central title banner "HACKER HOUSE" with spring-animated Devanagari **`गोवा`** overlay toggling to **`GOA`**.
   - Floating tropical sticker seals with anti-phase floating physics.

## Act II: Mode Selection
2. **Scene 02 — Mode Switcher**:
   - User chooses between **PFP Profile Frame** (1000x1000 square avatar for X / Telegram) and **Builder ID Pass** (1000x1400 full event badge with lanyard slot, stamp, QR code, and beach bag items).
   - Tactile spring transition indicator moves between tabs.

## Act III: Creation & Personalization
3. **Scene 03 — Photo Upload & Drag-to-Pan Canvas**:
   - Drag & drop image upload or 1-click sample avatars.
   - Live canvas preview updates smoothly.
   - On-canvas mouse/touch drag-to-pan, scroll wheel zoom, and keyboard nudge controls.
   - One-touch filter presets (Sunset Glow, Cyber Cyan, Warm Beach, Vivid Pop, B&W High Signal).
   - AI-assisted builder title generation via Gemini API.

## Act IV: Export & Signal Broadcast
4. **Scene 04 — Celebration & Sharing**:
   - 1-click Download PNG with tropical confetti particle explosion.
   - 1-click Copy Image to Clipboard.
   - Share Modal with live thumbnail preview and pre-populated X (Twitter) broadcast intent.
